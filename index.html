Real work · Case study

Gmail Customer Support Automation

Built in n8n. Reads every incoming support email, works out what the customer needs, and only asks a human to step in when it actually matters.

Every new email gets classified by intent — order status, refund, billing, general question, or spam — before anything else happens. Order and FAQ replies are drafted and sent automatically. Refund and billing cases are routed to a human for approval over Telegram before any reply goes out, and every case is logged to Google Sheets for tracking.
